# Huihutong Gate

An unofficial client that presents a user's Huihutong access credential and related room information.

## Language

**Power warning**:
A service-issued notice that the user's registered room balance is below the service-defined warning level.
_Avoid_: Balance, room balance

**Room balance**:
The latest monetary balance published by the billing system for an apartment and room selected by the user. It may be zero or negative, may change only a few times per day, and is not a live meter reading.
_Avoid_: Realtime billing fee, live balance, billing port, power warning
